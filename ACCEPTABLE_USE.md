# Acceptable use for the hosted service

The open-source software may be self-hosted under the Apache-2.0 license. Use of the public `getaroom.run` service is also subject to these operational boundaries.

Do not use the hosted service to:

- upload or distribute malware, phishing material, spam, or abusive automated traffic;
- coordinate unauthorized access, credential theft, or destructive activity;
- store or transmit unlawful content;
- deliberately evade room, request, size, or lifetime limits; or
- interfere with the service or other users.

The hosted service is offered on a best-effort basis without an uptime or data-recovery guarantee. Rooms may be rate-limited, closed, or blocked to protect the service. Do not use a room as the only copy of important information.

Treat files received through a room as untrusted. The hosted service does not promise malware scanning, content moderation, recovery, or permanent availability.
