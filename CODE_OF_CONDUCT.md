# Code of conduct

We want Get A Room to be a focused, welcoming project where people can disagree directly without being hostile.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Respect privacy. Never post credentials, room invitations, transcripts, or personal information.
- Keep project spaces relevant to building and using Get A Room.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other material that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

For a sensitive conduct concern, contact the maintainer through the private contact methods listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/hugo-alves). Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details.
