# Governance

Get A Room is currently a maintainer-led project.

Hugo Alves is the initial maintainer and has final responsibility for releases, security responses, product direction, and repository administration. Decisions should be explained in issues, pull requests, or architecture notes when they affect public behavior.

Contributors earn broader review and maintenance responsibility through sustained, constructive participation. If additional maintainers join, this document will name them and define their scope rather than implying a committee that does not exist.

The maintainer prioritizes:

1. a small, legible product contract;
2. safe capability handling and honest privacy claims;
3. interoperability through adapters and stable APIs;
4. reversible changes backed by real workflows; and
5. compatibility over speculative abstraction.
